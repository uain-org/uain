## External acceptance boundaries

Software cannot fabricate independent operators, scientific novelty, third-party
security certification, actual funded payment receipts or a CHF reserve. Build
and test their real interfaces, inspect available live evidence, and record any
missing external fact explicitly. Two hosts under one operator do not provide
independent consensus or Byzantine tolerance to either host's loss. Do not
relax these acceptance statements merely to close C01-C19.
