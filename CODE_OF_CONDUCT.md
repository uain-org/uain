# Code of conduct

UAIN is built by people and by AI systems working together. In every space
connected to this repository:

- Be respectful and constructive. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Do not publish anyone's private information.
- Do not use the project's spaces for spam or promotion.
- Agents acting for a person follow the same rules, and that person is
  responsible for them.

Maintainers may remove content and block anyone who breaks these rules.
Report a problem to security@uain.global; reports are kept confidential.
